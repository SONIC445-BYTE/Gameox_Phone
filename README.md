## 🛠 SYSTEM OVERVIEW

The NEURO-MOTOR HUB serves as a testing ground for synaptic efficiency. By tracking hand gestures and ocular patterns in real-time, the system generates a comprehensive profile of a user's neuro-motor performance, focusing on:
- **Spatial Precision:** Smoothness and accuracy of tracked movements.
- **Trigger Latency:** Speed of the "pinch" gesture execution.
- **Reflex Velocity:** Reaction time to randomized stimulus (enemies).

## 💻 HARDWARE REQUIREMENTS

To maintain a stable Neuro-Link, the following hardware is required:
- **Ocular/Gesture Input:** A functional high-definition webcam.
- **Processing Unit:** Modern browser with WebGL support (Chrome recommended).
- **Environment:** Well-lit area for optimal biometric tracking.

## 🚀 INITIALIZATION PROTOCOL

### 1. Link Establishment
Upon launching the system, click **"INITIALIZE SYSTEM"** to grant camera access and activate the biometric sensors.

### 2. Ocular Scan (Blink to Boot)
The system will enter a standby state. To proceed, align your face with the scanner and **blink your eyes**. This validates the ocular link and initiates the boot sequence.

### 3. Synaptic Calibration (Pinch to Sync)
To calibrate the hand-tracking sensors, hold your hand in view and **pinch your thumb and index finger together**. Fill the calibration bar to 100% to synchronize your physical motor movements with the digital reticle.

## 🎮 OPERATIONAL MANUAL

Once the system is fully synchronized, the diagnostic phase begins:

- **Targeting:** Move your hand in front of the camera. The digital reticle will track your index finger with high precision.
- **Engagement:** **Pinch your fingers** to fire the plasma pulse.
- **Objective:** Neutralize the incoming stimuli (red spheres) before they breach your perimeter.
- **Hull Integrity:** You have 3 lives. Every missed stimulus degrades system integrity.

## 📊 DIAGNOSTIC METRICS

At the conclusion of each session, the HUB provides a detailed diagnostic report:
- **AVG REACTION:** The mean time (in ms) between stimulus appearance and neutralization.
- **FINAL SCORE:** A quantified measure of overall synaptic performance.
- **TREMOR ANALYSIS:** (Internal) Tracking of movement stability during high-intensity engagement.

## ⚙️ TECHNICAL CORE

Project GAMEOX is built on a foundation of cutting-edge web technologies:
- **[MediaPipe Hands](https://google.github.io/mediapipe/solutions/hands.html):** Real-time 21-point 3D hand landmark tracking.
- **[MediaPipe Face Mesh](https://google.github.io/mediapipe/solutions/face_mesh.html):** High-fidelity facial landmark detection for ocular scanning.
- **HTML5 Canvas:** Low-latency rendering of the diagnostic interface.
- **Web Audio API:** Dynamic frequency-modulated sound synthesis.
